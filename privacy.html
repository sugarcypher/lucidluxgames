Privacy

Last updated October 10, 2026.

The games on this site do not ask for your name, email or any account, and they show no ads.

  • What is stored: your scores, settings and progress, kept only in your own browser on your own device.
  • What is sent anywhere: nothing. The games do not send your play data to us or to anyone else.
  • Deleting it: use "Reset scores, coins and profile" in the game's settings, or clear this site's data in your browser.

If a future version adds a shared leaderboard or any other feature that sends data, this page will say exactly what is sent and why before that version goes live.
